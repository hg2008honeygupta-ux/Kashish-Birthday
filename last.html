Dear Kashish, 💗

First of all, Happiest Birthdayyy, Kashishhh! ❤️ I hope today brings you as much happiness as you bring to the people around you. You genuinely deserve all the good things life has to offer.

It's funny how some people slowly become a part of your everyday memories without you even realising it. From our school days in 11th to Gagan Sir's classes, library ki backchodi, group conversations, lunch before Accounts, canteen moments and all those random jokes — there are so many little things that I still remember.

We might not have realised it back then, but those ordinary days became some really nice memories. I genuinely miss those days sometimes. The random conversations, the laughter and all the nonsense that somehow made everything more fun.

But there's one thing I will always remember. When I was admitted to the hospital and going through one of the lowest and most difficult phases of my life, you came to meet me. Not just once, but twice. And honestly, that meant a lot more than I can explain.

I still remember those roses and chocolates. It was such a thoughtful gesture, and your presence gave me comfort at a time when things were really difficult. I even remember those aunties staring and teasing me afterwards. 😭 That whole moment was something else!

Thank you for showing up when I needed support. Thank you for checking on me, for being there, and for making me feel that I wasn't alone. Maybe I didn't express it properly at that time, but I genuinely appreciate it. I'll always remember that kindness.

And Kashish, one more thing — whenever you feel low, whenever life gets overwhelming, or whenever you feel like you need someone to talk to, please remember that I'm always here for you. You don't have to deal with everything on your own. Whether you need someone to listen, someone to comfort you, or just someone to sit with you through a difficult day, you can always count on me. Just like you were there for me when I needed support, I hope I can be there for you too. Never hesitate to reach out. ❤️

And now, I really hope you're doing better too. I know you've been dealing with some health issues, and I genuinely wish you a speedy recovery. Please take care of yourself, get enough rest, and don't put unnecessary stress or pressure on yourself. Your health and peace matter.

You spend so much time caring for other people, comforting them and making sure they're okay. I hope you give yourself the same care and kindness. You deserve that too.

I hope this new year of your life brings you good health, happiness, success, countless reasons to smile and people who genuinely value you. May you achieve everything you've been working for and experience many beautiful moments ahead.

Keep making those funny faces, keep doing your unlimited bakloli, keep making random comments and keep being the caring person you are. Never lose that side of yourself.

Life gets busy, people get occupied with their own journeys, and things keep changing. But I'm genuinely glad we got to share these school days and create these memories together.

Once again, Happy Birthday, Kashish! Thank you for being a good friend and for being there during a time I'll never forget. I hope the coming year is kinder, healthier and happier for you. ❤️

Lots of good wishes, always. 🌷
